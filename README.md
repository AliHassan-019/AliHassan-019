## 👋 About Me

I’m **Ali Hassan**, an Embedded Systems Engineer building intelligent hardware and firmware solutions for IoT, automation, and robotics.

I combine deep knowledge of **microcontrollers, sensors, communication protocols, and PCB design** to deliver robust products that work in real-world conditions.

---

## 🚀 What I Bring

- Embedded firmware development for STM32, ESP32, and nRF platforms
- IoT system design with secure telemetry, MQTT, and low-power operation
- PCB layout, schematic design, and manufacturability engineering for mixed-signal hardware
- Sensor integration, control systems, and automation workflows
- Prototyping, testing, and product-level reliability engineering
